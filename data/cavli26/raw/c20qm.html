      C20QM LTE Cat 4 IoT Module

      Cavli is proud to present the C20QM, a series of LTE Cat 4 IoT modules, with emphasis on data speed/bandwidth, that support 3GPP release 10. Engineered in LGA form factor, it offers pin compatibility with the C10GS LTE Cat 1/Cat 4 and C10QM modules, allowing for flexible migration and interoperability. The module encompasses integrated GNSS, with Qualcomm® IZat™ location technology Gen8C Lite, providing precise and reliable positioning through GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou/Compass, Galileo, and QZSS.

      The C20QM supports VoLTE and SMS over IMS/SG/CS, along with a plethora of internet protocols and industry-standard interfaces. The module comes in two variants: C20QM-EA for the Europe and Asia markets, and C20QM-NA for the North America market. Additionally, with an optional integrated eSIM powered by the Cavli Hubble platform, it offers global connectivity, reducing time to market. The C20QM module is designed for IoT and M2M applications, such as asset tracking, e-mobility, data loggers, fleet management, and environmental monitoring.

        Go Beyond and Explore

        1.

        What GNSS capabilities does the C20QM module offer?

        The C20QM module integrates Qualcomm Location Suite Gen8C Lite technology, supporting GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo, and QZSS. This robust GNSS support ensures precise positioning for asset tracking, fleet management, and environmental monitoring.

        2.

        How does the C20QM module manage power consumption in IoT deployments?

        The C20QM module features advanced power management capabilities, including sleep modes. These features optimize power consumption, making the module ideal for IoT applications requiring medium throughput.

        3.

        Is the C20QM module integrated with the Cavli Hubble Platform for device management?

        Yes, the C20QM module is fully compatible with the Cavli Hubble Platform. This integration enables seamless device management, real-time monitoring, and remote diagnostics, reducing the complexity of global IoT deployments. For more information, visit the Cavli Hubble Platform.

        4.

        What are the interface options available on the C20QM module for IoT applications?

        The C20QM module offers a broad range of interfaces, including UART, USB 2.0, I2C, SPI, MIPI-DBI, GPIOs, and more. These interfaces support diverse IoT and M2M applications, facilitating integration with various sensors and peripherals for use cases in industries such as smart cities, automotive, and energy utilities.

        5.

        How does the C20QM module facilitate global connectivity and interoperability?

        The C20QM module is available in multiple variants tailored to different regions, including Europe, Asia, and North America. It also features an optional integrated eSIM, powered by the Cavli Hubble Platform, which simplifies global connectivity and ensures interoperability across regions, making it suitable for international IoT deployments.

        6.

        Is there additional peripheral connectivity offered on the C10QM module?

        The C10QM module is designed with an integrated MIPI-DBI interface, enabling out-of-the-box configuration for LCD displays to power use cases like two-wheeler clusters, etc. It is also bundled with transceiver support for WiFi and Bluetooth, enabling our customers to add additional wireless connectivity support for their smart solutions.

        7.

        Where can I purchase the C20QM module, its Evaluation Kit (EVK), and download technical brochures?

        You can purchase the C20QM module and its Evaluation Kits (EVKs) through the Cavli Wireless store or from authorized distributors. Visit the C20QM product page and select "ORDER SAMPLES" to purchase the module. If you need an Evaluation Kit, you can obtain it by clicking "ORDER DDKs". Detailed technical documents can be downloaded by navigating to "Downloadable Resources".
