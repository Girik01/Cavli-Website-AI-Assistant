      LTE Cat 1

      LTE Cat 1, or LTE Category 1, introduced in 3GPP Release 8, is a cellular technology designed for IoT applications that require medium data throughputs. It is positioned between high-bandwidth cellular technology like LTE Cat 4 and low-power wide area networks like NB-IoT and LTE-M1.

      SCROLL

      LTE Cat 1 technology is specifically designed to operate with medium data rates and moderate power consumption. LTE Cat 1 is the low-cost 4G cellular network standard, powering the broadest range of IoT applications. It supports Voice over LTE (VoLTE) and seamless handover, allowing continuous connectivity while switching between cell towers—crucial for mobile IoT applications.

      LTE Cat 1 IoT Applications: From Smart Metering to Wearables

      LTE Cat 1 technology is utilized in a wide range of applications where devices need a reliable connection rather than high-speed data transfer. It is an excellent choice for remote monitoring and data collection applications such as smart metering and critical equipment monitoring. Additionally, wearable health monitors, POS machines, and security systems can efficiently leverage LTE Cat 1 IoT Modules to establish a reliable, IoT-connected ecosystem.

      Highlights and Advantages of C-Series
      LTE Cat 1 IoT Modules

      C-Series LTE Cat 1 Modules support USB, and UART interfaces. The integrated LCD interface in C10QM empower applications like automotive telemetry units, EV charging stations, and environment monitoring.

      Integrated GNSS provides assistance of navigational constellations like GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, and Galileo for location tracking. In addition, the Qualcomm Location Suite in C11QM also employs multiple approaches like network-based positioning methods to deliver accurate location for IoT applications like asset tracking and fleet management.

      Go Beyond and Explore

      1.

      What is LTE Cat 1 Technology?

      LTE Category 1 (LTE Cat 1) is engineered for IoT applications that demand moderate throughput, supporting uplink speeds up to 5 Mbps and downlink speeds up to 10 Mbps. Unlike LTE Cat M1 and NB-IoT, which are optimized for low power and low data rate applications, LTE Cat 1 is ideal for applications requiring higher throughput and real-time data transmission such as interactive displays.

      2.

      How effective is LTE Cat 1 in mobile IoT applications requiring high mobility?

      LTE Cat 1 is highly effective in mobile IoT applications due to its robust handling of higher speed mobility. Its ability to maintain connection integrity, makes it suitable for applications like in-vehicle connectivity, fleet management systems, and other mobile operations that rely on consistent and reliable data transmission.

      3.

      What are the advantages of using LTE Cat 1 for Smart City infrastructure?

      LTE Cat 1 offers power efficiency and data throughput beneficial for diverse urban IoT deployments. It supports a variety of smart city applications  by facilitating reliable, medium-bandwidth data transmission and real-time responsiveness, essential for functions such as dynamic traffic systems and urban area surveillance.

      4.

      Are LTE Cat 1 modules future-proof with respect to the rollout of 5G technology?

      LTE Cat 1 modules are designed with forward compatibility in mind, ensuring they integrate seamlessly into 5G network ecosystems as these networks roll out globally. This forward compatibility protects IoT infrastructure investments from rapid technological changes, ensuring they remain relevant and functional over time.

      5.

      What is the difference of LTE Cat 1 from LTE Cat M1 and NB-IoT?

      LTE Cat 1, Cat M1, and NB-IoT each serve distinct IoT connectivity needs.

      • LTE Cat 1 is suitable for medium data rate applications. It offers moderate power consumption and good coverage using existing LTE networks.
      • LTE Cat M1 offers lower data rates and power consumption compared to LTE Cat 1. It provides better indoor coverage and lower latency, suitable for real-time IoT applications.
      • NB-IoT focuses on ultra-low power consumption and excellent coverage. It is suitable for applications that can tolerate higher latency and require long battery life.
